Privacy Policy

PartyPuzz  ·  Last updated: May 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PartyPuzz ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our mobile application (PartyPuzz). We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information ourselves. PartyPuzz does not require you to create an account or register in any way. All game data (player names, scores, preferences) is stored locally on your device only and is never sent to our servers.

2. Third-Party Services

PartyPuzz uses the following third-party services that may collect data independently:

Google AdMob (Advertising)

The free version of PartyPuzz displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use data such as:

  • Advertising ID (resettable in your device settings)
  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Ad interaction data (views, clicks)

This data is used to show relevant ads and measure their performance. You can opt out of personalised advertising at any time:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and Google's data disclosure for AdMob.

Google Play Billing (In-App Purchases)

The "Remove Ads" one-time purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment information. Purchase records are managed by Google and governed by Google Payments Privacy Notice.

3. Children's Privacy

PartyPuzz is intended for users aged 18 and older and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data through this app, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

4. Data Retention

Since we do not collect personal data ourselves, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Any data collected by third-party SDKs (AdMob, Google Play) is subject to their own retention policies.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your country of residence, you may have rights regarding your personal data (access, correction, deletion, portability, or restriction of processing). Because we do not hold your personal data, these rights are primarily exercisable directly with Google for the data they collect. Contact us at the address below and we will do our best to help.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

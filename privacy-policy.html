Privacy Policy

PartyPuzl  ·  Last updated: November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PartyPuzl ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our mobile application (PartyPuzl). We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information ourselves. PartyPuzl does not require you to create an account or register in any way, and it does not show advertisements. All game data (player names, player and party photos, parties, custom packs, and preferences) is stored locally on your device only and is never sent to our servers. Uninstalling the app deletes it; it is not backed up to the cloud or transferred to a new device.

Camera and Photos

PartyPuzl asks for camera access only so you can take photos of players and of your party during a game. You can decline and still play. Photos stay inside the app on your device. A photo is copied to your device's gallery (Pictures/PartyPuzl) only when you choose to save it.

2. Third-Party Services

PartyPuzl uses the following third-party service, which may collect data independently:

Google Play Billing (In-App Purchases)

The optional one-time purchase that unlocks the app's premium content is processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment information. Purchase records are managed by Google and governed by Google Payments Privacy Notice.

3. Children's Privacy

PartyPuzl is intended for users aged 18 and older and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data through this app, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

4. Data Retention

Since we do not collect personal data ourselves, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Any data collected by Google Play is subject to Google's own retention policies.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your country of residence, you may have rights regarding your personal data (access, correction, deletion, portability, or restriction of processing). Because we do not hold your personal data, these rights are primarily exercisable directly with Google for the data they collect. Contact us at the address below and we will do our best to help.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
